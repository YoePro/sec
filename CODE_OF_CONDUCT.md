# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss ideas, code and evidence rather than
attacking people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, publication of private
information without consent and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may moderate discussions, reject abusive contributions and restrict
participation proportionately. Report concerns privately to a project maintainer
through an established private contact channel; do not post sensitive details
in a public issue. A maintainer involved in a complaint should not adjudicate
it alone where an independent reviewer is available. Reports should be handled
with discretion, fairness and minimal disclosure. Retaliation is unacceptable.

This policy applies to project-managed collaboration spaces.
